# Changelog — EdgeResilienceLab

This changelog tracks **conceptual maturity progression**,  
not software releases and not calendar time.

EdgeResilienceLab evolves when **architectural reasoning becomes clearer, safer, and more governable**.

Each level represents a **stabilized maturity state** of the repository.

## Maturity Level 1 — Bounded Adaptive Intelligence

### Established
- Adaptive learning governance boundary
- Human validation gates for learning
- Rollback enforcement
- AI advisory-only semantics

### Purpose
Enable intelligence augmentation without uncontrolled autonomy.

---

## Maturity Level 2 — Diagrammatic and Visual Reasoning

### Established
- C4 context and container views
- SysML state and authority models
- Diagram governance READMEs

### Purpose
Provide visual artifacts for architecture discussion and audits.

---

## Maturity Level 3 — Publication and Operationalization

### Established
- Automation scaffolding scripts
- Documentation validation workflows
- CODEOWNERS governance
- Clean publication and reset pipelines

### Purpose
Make the repository reproducible, navigable, and publication-ready.

---

## Change Policy

- Any new concept must increase maturity, not complexity
- Authority guarantees may not be weakened
- No change may introduce implicit autonomy
- Governance-altering changes require ADR updates
- Evidence generation must remain reconstructable

---

## Note

EdgeResilienceLab evolves by **depth of understanding**,  
not by speed of iteration.

Stability of reasoning is the primary design constraint.
