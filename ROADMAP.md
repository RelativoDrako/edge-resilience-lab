The roadmap expresses maturity of architectural capabilities, not delivery schedules.

## Roadmap Governance

Future evolution requires:  

- New ADR  
- Human authority approval  
- Audit trail  
- No implicit authority transfer

## Integrated Repository Maturity Extension

The public repository now also tracks maturity across complementary architecture-support layers.

### Integration Lane A — Governed AI Reference Layer

- Governed AI case study integrated
- Authority and governance-flow materials available
- AI advisory-only semantics reinforced

**Status:** Integrated as public conceptual support

### Integration Lane B — Publication Automation Layer

- CI/CD automation block integrated
- Clean and standard deployment manuals included
- Documentation validation workflow preserved

**Status:** Integrated as publication-support tooling

### Integration Lane C — Device Discovery and Evidence Layer

- Multiplatform Linux and Windows inventory lanes integrated
- Evidence-first reporting methodology included
- IP and publication-boundary material preserved

**Status:** Integrated as public tooling reference

### Integration Governance

Future additions must:

- preserve explicit authority boundaries;
- avoid weakening AI advisory-only semantics;
- maintain Work in Progress positioning;
- preserve evidence-first and publication-boundary discipline;
- avoid exposing implementation-sensitive recovery internals before validation.
